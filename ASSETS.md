Mode: built-in imagegen, tiga permintaan, tanpa salinan aset Educaplay.

## assets/frog.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG game character for an Indonesian classroom frog-on-lily-pad multiple-choice game. One friendly green cartoon frog, front view from slightly above, sitting with bent hind legs and big eyes, green body, cream belly, small purple explorer backpack and visible purple straps. Genuinely transparent background with alpha; no opaque fill or drawn checkerboard. Original cheerful clean bold flat 2D illustration with modest shading, smooth vector-like edges, not photorealistic. Square canvas, isolated fully visible centered character filling about 80% of canvas. One frog only, no lily pad/environment/text/letters/logo/watermark/Educaplay branding.

## assets/count-3.png, assets/count-2.png, assets/count-1.png

Use case: stylized-concept countdown. Transparent PNG game assets for the 3-2-1 pre-game countdown sequence. Vibrant 3D cartoon numbers in glossy light green (hijau muda / lime green) with bright lime highlights, soft bevels, specular white reflections, rounded bubble contours, and a dark outline. Displayed against a dark/black backdrop during countdown before the home game pond appears. Processed with genuine alpha channel transparency for smooth scaling and overlay presentation.
